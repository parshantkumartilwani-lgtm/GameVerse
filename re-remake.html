About the Game

Resident Evil 4 Remake is a modern reimagining of the 2005 survival horror classic, developed and published by Capcom. You play as Leon S. Kennedy, a government agent sent to rescue the president’s daughter from a mysterious cult in a remote village.

Game Details

  • Developer: Capcom
  • Publisher: Capcom
  • Release Date: March 24, 2023
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S
  • Genre: Survival Horror, Action-Adventure

Gameplay

The remake features overhauled graphics, refined combat mechanics, expanded story elements, and enhanced horror atmosphere. Experience intense third-person action, strategic resource management, and terrifying encounters in a beautifully reimagined world.
