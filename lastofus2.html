About the Game

The Last of Us Part II Remastered is a haunting and emotional sequel developed by Naughty Dog and remastered for PC by Iron Galaxy Studios. Set in a post-pandemic world, it follows Ellie and Abby through a gripping narrative of revenge, trauma, and survival. The PC version features enhanced graphics, performance options, and a new roguelike mode called No Return.
