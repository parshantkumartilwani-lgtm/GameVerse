About the Game

F1 22 is the official video game of the 2022 FIA Formula One World Championship, developed by Codemasters and published by EA Sports. Experience the new era of Formula 1 with redesigned cars, updated rules, and immersive race-day experiences.

Game Details

  • Developer: Codemasters
  • Publisher: EA Sports
  • Release Date: July 1, 2022
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One
  • Genre: Racing, Simulation, Sports

Gameplay

Race with the official teams and drivers across all 22 circuits of the 2022 season. F1 22 introduces adaptive AI, VR support (PC), and the new F1 Life hub for customizing your lifestyle off the track.
