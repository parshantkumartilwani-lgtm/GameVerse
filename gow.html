About the Game

God of War: Ragnarok is an epic action-adventure developed by Santa Monica Studio and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Released in November 2022, it continues the emotional journey of Kratos and Atreus as they confront gods, monsters, and fate itself across the Nine Realms.

Game Details

  • Developer: Santa Monica Studio
  • Publisher: Sony Interactive Entertainment
  • Release Date: November 9, 2022
  • Platforms: PS4, PS5, PC (2024)
  • Genre: Action-Adventure

Gameplay

Wield the Leviathan Axe and Blades of Chaos in brutal combat, solve ancient puzzles, and explore breathtaking realms. Every decision shapes Kratos' legacy as he battles destiny and seeks redemption in a world on the brink of Ragnarok.
