About the Game

Grand Theft Auto V is Rockstar Games’ award-winning open-world action-adventure, set in the sprawling city of Los Santos and its surrounding countryside. Follow the intertwined stories of Michael, Franklin, and Trevor as they navigate crime, corruption, and chaos in a satirical take on modern America.

Game Details

  • Developer: Rockstar North
  • Publisher: Rockstar Games
  • Release Date: April 14, 2015 (PC)
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One
  • Genre: Action, Open World, Crime

Gameplay

Switch between three protagonists, pull off cinematic heists, explore a massive world, and dive into GTA Online for multiplayer mayhem. Customize vehicles, buy properties, and experience a living city full of opportunities and danger.
