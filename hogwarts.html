About the Game

Hogwarts Legacy is an immersive open-world action RPG set in the 1800s Wizarding World, developed by Avalanche Software and published by Warner Bros. Games. Players assume the role of a student at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry who holds the key to an ancient secret threatening the magical realm.

Game Details

  • Developer: Avalanche Software
  • Publisher: Warner Bros. Games
  • Release Date: February 10, 2023 (PC)
  • Platforms: PC
  • Genre: Action RPG, Open World, Fantasy

Gameplay

Explore iconic locations like Hogwarts, Hogsmeade, and the Forbidden Forest. Learn spells, brew potions, tame magical beasts, and uncover hidden mysteries. Customize your character, choose your house, and shape your legacy in a richly detailed magical world.
