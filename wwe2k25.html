About the Game

WWE 2K25 is the latest installment in the WWE 2K series, developed by Visual Concepts and published by 2K Sports. Featuring a massive roster of WWE Superstars and Legends, new match types, and enhanced visuals, it delivers the most immersive wrestling experience yet.

Gameplay

WWE 2K25 introduces new animations, MyGM upgrades, Universe Mode enhancements, and a deeper MyRise story mode. Customize arenas, create your own Superstar, and dominate the ring in online and local multiplayer.
