About the Game

Forza Horizon 5 is an open-world racing game developed by Playground Games and published by Xbox Game Studios. Set in a stunning recreation of Mexico, it features dynamic weather, diverse biomes, and hundreds of customizable cars.

Game Details

  • Developer: Playground Games
  • Publisher: Xbox Game Studios
  • Release Date: November 9, 2021
  • Platforms: PC, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One
  • Genre: Racing, Open World

Gameplay

Explore vibrant landscapes, participate in seasonal events, and race solo or online. Forza Horizon 5 introduces the Horizon Arcade, EventLab, and a deeper car tuning system for ultimate driving freedom.
