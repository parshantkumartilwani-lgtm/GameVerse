About the Game

Elden Ring is a critically acclaimed open-world action RPG developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. Set in the mysterious Lands Between, players embark on a journey to become the Elden Lord, guided by lore crafted in collaboration with George R. R. Martin.

Game Details

  • Developer: FromSoftware
  • Publisher: Bandai Namco Entertainment
  • Release Date: February 25, 2022
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One
  • Genre: Action RPG, Open World, Fantasy

Gameplay

Explore vast landscapes, deadly dungeons, and towering castles. Customize your character, master combat styles, and uncover deep lore through exploration and discovery. Elden Ring offers freedom, challenge, and a hauntingly beautiful world filled with secrets.
