About the Game

Assetto Corsa is a highly acclaimed racing simulator developed by Kunos Simulazioni. Known for its realistic physics, laser-scanned tracks, and modding support, it offers an immersive driving experience for motorsport enthusiasts.

Game Details

  • Developer: Kunos Simulazioni
  • Publisher: 505 Games
  • Release Date: December 19, 2014
  • Platforms: PC, PS4, Xbox One
  • Genre: Racing Simulation

Gameplay

Assetto Corsa features a wide range of licensed cars and tracks, advanced tire and suspension modeling, and support for VR and racing wheels. It’s a favorite among sim racers for its realism and community-driven mods.
