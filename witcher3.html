About the Game

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is a critically acclaimed open-world RPG developed by CD Projekt Red. You play as Geralt of Rivia, a monster hunter for hire, navigating war-torn kingdoms, political intrigue, and supernatural threats in a richly detailed fantasy world.

Game Details

  • Developer: CD Projekt Red
  • Publisher: CD Projekt
  • Release Date: May 19, 2015
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch
  • Genre: Action RPG, Fantasy, Open World

Gameplay

Explore vast regions, battle deadly creatures, and make choices that shape the world around you. The game features deep storytelling, dynamic combat, and memorable characters — including the expansions Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine.
