About the Game

Alan Wake II is a psychological survival horror game developed by Remedy Entertainment and published by Epic Games Publishing. Released in October 2023, it continues the story of troubled writer Alan Wake as he tries to escape a nightmarish dimension while FBI agent Saga Anderson investigates ritualistic murders in the Pacific Northwest.

Game Details

  • Developer: Remedy Entertainment
  • Publisher: Epic Games Publishing
  • Release Date: October 27, 2023
  • Platforms: PC
  • Genre: Survival Horror, Psychological Thriller

Gameplay

Alan Wake II blends intense third-person combat with atmospheric exploration and narrative-driven horror. Players switch between Alan and Saga, uncovering twisted mysteries and confronting supernatural threats using light-based mechanics and investigative tools. The game features cinematic storytelling, haunting environments, and nonlinear progression.
