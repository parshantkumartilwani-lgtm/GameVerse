About the Game

Cyberpunk 2077 is a sprawling open-world RPG developed by CD Projekt Red, set in the neon-lit dystopia of Night City. You play as V, a mercenary navigating a world of cybernetic enhancements, corporate warfare, and digital consciousness.

Game Details

  • Developer: CD Projekt Red
  • Publisher: CD Projekt
  • Release Date: December 10, 2020
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One
  • Genre: Action RPG, Sci-Fi, Open World

Gameplay

Customize your character, choose your backstory, and explore a city teeming with life and danger. Engage in hacking, combat, and dialogue-driven quests. The game features branching narratives, immersive environments, and the acclaimed Phantom Liberty expansion.
