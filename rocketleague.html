About the Game

Rocket League is a fast-paced sports-action game developed by Psyonix, combining soccer with rocket-powered vehicles. Play solo or team up online in thrilling matches that blend strategy, speed, and aerial stunts.

Game Details

  • Developer: Psyonix
  • Publisher: Epic Games
  • Release Date: July 7, 2015
  • Platforms: PC, PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch
  • Genre: Sports, Racing, Multiplayer

Gameplay

Customize your car, compete in ranked matches, and master aerial goals in futuristic arenas. Rocket League supports cross-platform play, seasonal events, and esports tournaments.
