About the Game

Uncharted: Legacy of Thieves Collection brings together two critically acclaimed single-player adventures: Uncharted 4: A Thief’s End and Uncharted: The Lost Legacy. Developed by Naughty Dog and remastered by Iron Galaxy Studios, this collection follows Nathan DrakeChloe Frazer as they confront their pasts and chase legendary treasures across the globe.
